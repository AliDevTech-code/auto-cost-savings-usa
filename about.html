ABOUT THIS SITE

Car costs deserve a clearer explanation.

Auto Cost & Savings USA is an independent, practical resource for people trying to understand the everyday costs of owning and driving a vehicle in the United States.

Why we built it

It is easy to focus on the sticker price or monthly payment and overlook fuel, charging, maintenance and the smaller expenses that build up over time. Our goal is to make those numbers easier to explore with simple tools and straightforward explanations.

How to use our calculators

Enter your own assumptions and treat the result as a starting point. We aim to explain what each estimate includes and what it leaves out. We do not have access to your lender, utility, insurer or vehicle records, and we do not provide personalized quotes.

Our approach

We prefer clear assumptions, useful examples and links to official sources where current information matters. We update content as we can, but rates, prices, vehicle specifications and regulations can change. Please verify important details before making a financial or purchasing decision.
