How to estimate a car payment before visiting a dealer

A monthly payment can look manageable while the total amount borrowed tells a different story. Take a few minutes to understand the inputs before comparing offers.

Start with the amount financed

Begin with the vehicle price, then account for your down payment and any trade-in value. Taxes, title, registration, dealer fees and optional products can change the amount financed. Ask for an itemized out-the-door price so you are not comparing incomplete figures.

Understand APR and loan term

APR represents the annual cost of borrowing expressed as a rate. The loan term is how many months you will make payments. A longer term can reduce the monthly payment but may increase total interest and keep you in debt longer.

Compare the full offer

  • Compare APR, not just the monthly payment.
  • Check the total amount financed and total of payments.
  • Review fees, add-ons and any prepayment conditions.
  • Get financing quotes before you visit if possible.

Leave room for the rest of the car budget

Your payment is not the full cost of driving. Add insurance, fuel or charging, maintenance, parking, registration and likely repairs to see whether the vehicle fits your monthly budget.
